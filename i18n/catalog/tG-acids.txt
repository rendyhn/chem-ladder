<h3>Buffers</h3>
<p>A <b>buffer</b> keeps the pH almost constant when a little acid or base is added. It contains a weak acid and its conjugate base (e.g. $\ce{CH3COOH}$ + $\ce{CH3COONa}$) or a weak base and its conjugate acid ($\ce{NH3}$ + $\ce{NH4Cl}$). Added $\ce{H+}$ is taken up by $\ce{A-}$; added $\ce{OH-}$ is neutralised by $\ce{HA}$.</p>

<p>The <b>solubility product</b> is the equilibrium constant (the solid is left out): $$K_{sp} = [\ce{Ag+}][\ce{Cl-}] = 1.8 \times 10^{-10}\ (25\ °\mathrm{C})$$ For a salt $\ce{M_xA_y}$ with solubility $s$ mol/L: $K_{sp} = (xs)^x(ys)^y$.
